## Cadence & approach

- Post 1-4 and 6-8 can go up now using existing assets — no new production needed.
- Post 5 (before/after clip) is the highest-value one to actually make time for — it's the real differentiator (preview-before-commit) shown, not just described.
- Space these out (every 2-3 days, not all at once) rather than dumping the whole backlog in one day — a brand-new account posting 8 times in 24 hours reads as spammy/bot-like.
- No hard-sell CTA needed on every post ("try it free," "link in bio, install now") — app isn't installable yet, so the honest CTA throughout is "see the demo" / "here's what it does," consistent with the tone already set in the outreach emails and community posts.
- Once a couple of the real leads from [outreach-list-and-messages.md](outreach-list-and-messages.md) reply or engage, consider tagging/crediting them (with permission) in a follow-up post — social proof from a real dealer beats another feature screenshot.
