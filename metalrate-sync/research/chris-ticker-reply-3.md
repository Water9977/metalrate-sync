Hey Chris,

Quick answers to everything you asked:

**SKUs — you don't need any.** Saw your note, didn't realize you had none. Fixed it on our end so it's a non-issue: download your catalog as a CSV from inside the app, fill in a weight + formula per row, re-upload. No SKUs to create, nothing to retype — price stays live from there.

**Ticker on your theme** — Brooklyn won't show it under "Add section" (a Shopify vintage-theme thing, not us), so instead: Theme editor → **App embeds** (bottom-left icon) → toggle "Metal Price Ticker" on → save. Shows up automatically above your header, full width. No code, no pasting.

**One heads up**: turn off live pricing in your current app for anything you move to us — otherwise both apps fight over the same price field. Do it product by product as you go, no rush.

Take your time installing, no pressure. Shout if anything looks off.
